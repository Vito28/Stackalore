A graph represents entities as vertices and relationships as edges. Unlike a tree, a graph may contain cycles, multiple paths, and disconnected regions.

## Two common representations

| Representation | Space | Best fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adjacency list | `O(V + E)` | Sparse graphs and neighbor traversal |
| Adjacency matrix | `O(V²)` | Dense graphs and fast edge checks |

## Directed and weighted edges

A directed edge has an orientation, such as one account following another. A weighted edge adds a cost or distance, such as travel time between cities.

<Callout type="info" title="Before choosing an algorithm">
  Ask whether the graph is directed, weighted, cyclic, and potentially disconnected. Those properties determine which traversal or shortest-path algorithm is valid.
</Callout>
